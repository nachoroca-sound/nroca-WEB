C. Tangana × Bershka

ProjectC. Tangana × Bershka
DirectorMariano Schoendorff
ClientLittle Planet
Year2021

Overview

C. Tangana × Bershka was an international campaign produced by Little Spain to launch the collaboration celebrating El Madrileño. My role focused on shaping the project's sonic identity through custom sound design and audio post-production, carefully supporting its cinematic visual language without overshadowing the music. Every sound was crafted to enhance movement, rhythm, and storytelling, bringing an extra layer of energy and realism to the campaign while maintaining the refined aesthetic expected from an international fashion production.

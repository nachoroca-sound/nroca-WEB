Overview

Alegal's Not Another F*cking Restaurant is a short-form commercial directed by Sergio Miña, created to introduce the restaurant through a cinematic and irreverent narrative. The campaign reflects Alegal's concept as a restaurant and nightlife space built around a distinctive, theatrical atmosphere. I was responsible for the sound design and final mix, building the sonic world of the piece from the ground up. The sound design was shaped around the film's fast-paced editing and playful attitude, using detailed effects, transitions and rhythmic elements to reinforce the visual storytelling and give the commercial the feel of a short film.

Path of Exile 2 | Huntress — Sound Design Demo

Overview

These videos were created as part of the final stage of the recruitment process at Grinding Gear Games. For this sound design test, I redesigned the abilities of two distinct character classes, carefully crafting each skill to respect the game's dark, grounded aesthetic and seamlessly fit within its established audio direction. The focus was on creating impactful, readable, and immersive abilities that felt authentic to the world of Path of Exile 2 while showcasing my approach to AAA gameplay sound design.

Overview

For Pepe Jeans' London Calling campaign, I worked across more than 20 pieces, handling the music editing and mixing for the campaign's different assets. The work involved adapting and refining the music across multiple formats and edits, ensuring consistency and impact across the full campaign while maintaining the intended character of the original track. I also provided sound design for several of the pieces, creating and integrating additional sound elements to complement the visuals and enhance the overall audiovisual experience.

Year2026

Overview

The Zibbo Show is a dark dystopian dice-building roguelike set inside a twisted television game show where every challenge is a fight for survival. I joined the project as Audio Director, taking responsibility for defining its overall sonic identity. The soundscape combines eerie environmental tones with the focused tension of a high-stakes game show, using rising pitches and casino-inspired cues to build anticipation and excitement without becoming overwhelming. From the studio ambience to the mechanical personality of its robotic host, every sound reinforces the contrast between its vibrant TV-show aesthetic and the disturbing reality behind it. The game's reveal trailer was also featured by IGN.

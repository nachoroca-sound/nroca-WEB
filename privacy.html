2. What data we collect

This website does not include a contact form and does not automatically collect any personal data. If you contact us using the email address on the contact page, your email client sends your message directly to us. In that case, we receive only the information you choose to include (typically your name, email address, and message). No data is collected passively while you browse the site.

3. Cookies and third-party services

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics of any kind.

However, certain pages embed content from third-party services:

We have no control over the data practices of these third-party services.

4. How we use your data

Any personal data you send us via email is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not share it with third parties, use it for marketing, or retain it beyond the time needed to address your message.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Authority (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos — AEPD) at aepd.es. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at nroca@whoestudio.com.
